Game Book


This game is meant to be played using both the desktop and the books provided (located in the clear trays).

Draw or write in the book when prompted by the game.

There will be a 2 minute timer for you to complete the prompt in the book before continuing the game.

After completing the game, you may either take the book with you as a personal record, or leave it behind as part of the installation to share with others.

Text-Based Interaction


Type short 2-3 word commands and press the enter key.

Examples of commands include:

  • go north
  • go south
  • go east
  • go west
  • look
  • examine _
  • open _
  • pick up _

    • Words that are bolded and capitalized may be interacted with and can be used in a command.

    Type 'help' to see what commands can be used within a room.
